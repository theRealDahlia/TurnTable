# Logos

- `qobuz.svg`: Qobuz's site icon, downloaded from https://static.qobuz.com/images/favicon/favicon.svg. The mark is Qobuz's trademark, used here only to identify the service.
- Spotify, YouTube, and SoundCloud marks come from the `simple-icons` package (see `ProviderLogo.tsx`).
- `spotify-full-white.svg` and `spotify-full-black.svg`: Spotify's full logo (icon and wordmark), unmodified, from the official pack at https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design (`2024-spotify-full-logo.zip`, files `Full_Logo_White_RGB.svg` and `Full_Logo_Black_RGB.svg`). Spotify's guidelines allow the green logo only on pure black or white, so the app uses the monochrome versions, white on its dark theme and black on its light one. Never recolor, stretch, rotate or crop them, keep clear space around them, and keep the full logo at least 70 px wide. Used under the Spotify Developer Terms.
